Your day at a glance

One ring for the whole day.

See when you slept, when you were locked and when you spent a long stretch on your phone, all on a single 24-hour ring.

  • Locked and unlocked states. The home screen shows how long until the next lock, or how long this session has been running.
  • Hold to lock, hold to unlock. A long press keeps it deliberate, not a reflex.
  • Emergency unlock. A limited number each month for when you really need your phone.
Modes and schedules

A mode for every part of your day.

Study, sleep, focus. Keep as many modes as you need and switch between them without rebuilding your block list.

  • Apps and websites. Block both, so a distraction does not just move to the browser.
  • Schedules by day and time. Pick the days and hours, and Atoll locks and releases automatically.
  • Relock delay. If you unlock early, it locks again after the delay you choose.
Stats

Weekly, monthly and yearly views show your focus time, how often you unlocked and which days you kept your streak.

  • Focus time and trends. Compare this month with last month.
  • Day-by-day heatmap. Spot the days that go well and the ones that slip.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

When is Atoll available?

Atoll is in development for iPhone. Leave your email above and we will let you know when it launches. You can also reach us at hello@tryatoll.app.

Do I need to buy NFC tags?

No. You can register an NFC card you already own, such as a transit card. Holding the button to unlock works without any card at all.

What if I need my phone in an emergency?

Atoll has an emergency unlock with a limited number of uses each month, so you are never fully locked out.

Which apps and sites can it block?

You choose them. Atoll uses Apple's Screen Time framework to block the apps you pick, plus websites you list.

Does it work on Android?

Not yet. Atoll is built for iPhone first.
