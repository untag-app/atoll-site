Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Atoll ("we", "us") is an iPhone app that blocks distracting apps and websites on a schedule. We are based in Sydney, Australia. This page explains what we collect, why, who helps us handle it, and how you can have it removed. You can reach us any time at hello@tryatoll.app.

The app is not released yet. Right now we only collect the email addresses people leave on this website. The sections about the app describe what it will do once it is available, and we will update this page if that changes.

1. This website

If you sign up for launch news, we store your email address and the time you signed up. We use it to tell you about Atoll, such as the launch or a beta invitation. We do not sell it, share it for advertising or use it for anything else. To be removed, email us and we will delete it.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see aggregate numbers such as how many people visit and which pages they open. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other sites. Our hosting and infrastructure providers may also log technical data such as your IP address when you visit. We do not use any of this to identify you.

2. The app: what we collect

  • Account. You sign in with Apple. We receive a user identifier, and the name and email address Apple shares with us. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email", we only see Apple's private relay address.
  • What you set up. Your modes (name, icon, how you unlock), the websites you chose to block, a protected record of the apps you chose to block, your schedules, and the NFC cards you register (the card's ID number, the name and icon you give it, how often you used it and when you last did).
  • Lock history. For each lock: when it started and ended, how it ended (card, hold or emergency unlock), what started it, the mode's name at the time, and how many apps and sites it blocked.
  • Daily summary. Rough time ranges of when you likely slept and when you used your phone for a long stretch while unlocked, so your stats survive a reinstall. We do not store which apps you used in those ranges.
  • Settings. Your preferences, such as break length, Strict mode and notification options, and how many emergency unlocks you have used this month.

3. What stays on your phone

Atoll reads Screen Time data on your phone, with your permission, to draw your stats. How long you used each app stays on the phone and is not uploaded. Apple also keeps the apps you block in an opaque form that Atoll itself cannot read.

4. How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, keep your modes and history, show your stats, restore your data on a new phone and remember your settings. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

5. Who handles it for us

  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, the App Store, and Screen Time. Their own privacy policy applies to what they handle.
  • Supabase stores your account and the app data above for us. The data is held in South Korea (Seoul), and each person can reach only their own records.
  • Cloudflare runs our website's DNS, the service that records launch sign-ups, and the privacy-friendly visit statistics, and forwards mail sent to our address.
  • GitHub hosts this website.
  • Resend sends email on our behalf, such as launch news.

Because of these providers, your information may be processed outside the country where you live, including in South Korea and the United States. We do not share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting it

Launch sign-ups are kept until you ask us to remove them or we have sent the launch news and you no longer want to hear from us. App data is kept until you delete your account. You can do that inside the app from your account in Settings. It removes your account and all the data above from our servers and clears the app's data on your phone. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

7. Your choices and rights

You can see and change your modes, schedules and cards in the app. Depending on where you live, you may also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how we use it. Email us and we will reply within 30 days. We handle personal information in line with applicable privacy law, including Australia's Privacy Act 1988 where it applies. If you think we got it wrong, you can contact your local privacy regulator, such as the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

8. Permissions the app asks for

  • Screen Time access lets Atoll block apps and websites and read how long you use your phone. Without it, blocking and stats do not work. You can switch it off in iOS Settings.
  • Notifications warn you before a lock starts and when a break ends. They are optional.
  • NFC lets Atoll read the ID number of the cards you choose to register. It never writes anything to your cards.

9. Children

Atoll is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email.
