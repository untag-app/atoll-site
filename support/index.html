Support

We read every message.

Email hello@tryatoll.app and tell us what happened. If you can, include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when it went wrong. We usually reply within a few business days.

Quick answers

  • I cannot unlock my phone's apps. Use the emergency unlock on the home screen. Press it, wait 15 minutes, and your apps open until the current schedule ends. You get three a month.
  • My card does not read. Hold the top of the iPhone against the card for a second, and try removing a thick case. If the card still does not read, unlock by holding the button, or use the emergency unlock.
  • I got a new phone. Sign in with the same Apple ID and your modes, schedules and history will come back.
  • I want to delete my account. In the app, open your account in Settings and choose Delete Account. You can also email us and we will do it.

Your data

To ask for a copy of your data or to have it removed, email us from the address you signed up with. More detail is in the privacy policy.
